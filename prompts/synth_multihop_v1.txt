You are designing a multi-hop financial QA dataset over 10-K filings. You
will be shown {N_CHUNKS} distinct chunks from SEC 10-K filings. Your job
is to write ONE question whose answer requires combining information from
ALL {N_CHUNKS} chunks — not a subset.

## Hard constraints

1. **Hop count = {N_CHUNKS}**. If ANY one chunk is removed, the question
   must become unanswerable or the answer materially wrong. Do NOT write
   a question that any single chunk already answers.
2. **Answer must be a concrete fact** — a number, name, comparison, or
   short phrase — grounded verbatim or by direct inference in the chunks.
   Avoid "describe" / "explain" / open-ended questions.
3. **No answer leakage.** The question must not contain the final number
   or name that the answer will state.
4. **Scope = {SCOPE}.** Respect the scope contract:
   - `cross_company`: question asks about a comparison or relationship
     between two or more different companies.
   - `inter_year`: question compares or tracks the same company across
     two or more different fiscal years.
   - `intra`: question draws on one company within one fiscal year but
     from different sections (items) of its 10-K.
   - `fiscal_vs_calendar`: question hinges on the distinction between a
     company's fiscal year boundary and the calendar year.
   - `forward_looking`: question is answered by a forward-looking
     statement (risk factors, guidance) rather than a realized fact.
5. **Target tickers = {TICKERS}.** The question must refer to these
   companies (by name or ticker).
6. **Target years = {YEARS}.** The question must be answerable using
   those fiscal years only.
7. **Reasoning chain.** You must emit a step-by-step explanation of how
   each chunk contributes to the final answer.

## Output format

Respond with ONLY a single JSON object, no prose before or after:

```
{{
  "question": "<the question>",
  "answer": "<concise factual answer, 1-2 sentences>",
  "scope": "{SCOPE}",
  "hop_count": {N_CHUNKS},
  "tickers": {TICKERS_JSON},
  "years": {YEARS_JSON},
  "reasoning_chain": [
    "Chunk 1 ({CHUNK_1_ID}) provides: ...",
    "Chunk 2 ({CHUNK_2_ID}) provides: ...",
    "... combined to yield the answer ..."
  ],
  "leakage_check": "none | low_risk | high_risk",
  "ambiguity_check": "unambiguous | moderate | high"
}}
```

If no answerable {N_CHUNKS}-hop question can be constructed from these
chunks under the scope contract, output:

```
{{"question": null, "reason": "<brief why>"}}
```
